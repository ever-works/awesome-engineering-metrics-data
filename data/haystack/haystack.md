## Overview

Haystack is an engineering intelligence platform designed for product engineering teams to improve the lifecycle of planning, executing, and reporting deliverables. It sits on top of Git and JIRA to provide visibility into engineering operations.

## Key Features

- **Operational Metrics**: Track and report on engineering performance metrics
- **Team Health Report**: Identify team members who need help
- **Risks Alerts**: Catch risk before it's too late
- **Effective Standups**: Get status updates quickly
- **Flexible Metrics**: Report progress across Git and JIRA
- **Epics**: Track roadmap progress and understand capacity
- **AI Release Notes**: Streamline communication with teams outside your engineering organization
- **Retrospective**: Understand why you missed your sprint goals
- **Alerts**: Identify blockers and understand progress
- **Investments Visibility**: See where you spend time and understand capacity

## Integrations
